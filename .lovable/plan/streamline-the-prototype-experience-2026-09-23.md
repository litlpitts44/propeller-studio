# Streamline the Prototype Experience

## Goal
Make Prototype 2 easy for a participant to explore without facilitator guidance. Lead with a concise overview, reduce competing navigation, and keep evidence strength visible throughout.

## Changes

### 1. Create a clearer participant journey
- Open on a focused **Overview** rather than dropping users directly into the scenario runner.
- Give the experience three primary steps: **Overview**, **Try a scenario**, and **Review insights**.
- Move Workflow, RACI, Decisions & Rules, Measures & Risks, and technical details into a quieter secondary “Explore the blueprint” area.

### 2. Simplify the overview
- Show the prototype’s purpose, recommended format, key things to test, and a prominent **Start a scenario** action.
- Keep only the most useful summary counts and remove repeated strategy, metadata, and explanatory blocks.
- Present assumptions and open questions as concise validation notes rather than full-width competing sections.

### 3. Make scenarios self-guided
- Replace the current stacked instructions, scenario buttons, context panel, and interaction card with one guided flow.
- Let participants choose a scenario, see the current role and situation, then make one clear choice.
- Move example data, backstage activity, evidence notes, and path history into expandable supporting details.
- Add clear progress and back/restart controls so participants always know where they are.

### 4. Keep evidence strength understandable
- Retain a compact, persistent legend for **Confirmed**, **Proposed**, **Assumed**, and **Unresolved**.
- Show the relevant status beside each scenario step and choice without repeating long explanations.
- Use plain participant-facing labels while preserving the current underlying evidence logic.

### 5. Reduce version and page noise
- Replace the large prototype-history button strip with a compact version selector.
- Keep the current prototype name, blueprint version, and timestamp visible in one restrained header.
- Preserve access to every existing deliverable and prototype version without making them compete with the primary task.

## Validation
- Test the overview-to-scenario journey with Prototype 2 at desktop and narrow widths.
- Confirm all existing outputs remain accessible, scenario choices still work, and evidence labels remain accurate.
- Check that no controls overlap, text truncates incorrectly, or navigation becomes ambiguous.
